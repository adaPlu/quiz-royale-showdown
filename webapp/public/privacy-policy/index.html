Quiz Royale Showdown Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quiz Royale Showdown collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Quiz Royale Showdown mobile game, website, and related online services.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the game, we may collect:

  • Account information, such as username, email address, hashed password, profile details, and authentication tokens.
  • Guest session information, such as a temporary guest identifier, display name, session token, and expiration status.
  • Gameplay information, such as room codes, match participation, answers, scores, power-up use, experience, rankings, achievements, friends, and leaderboard entries.
  • Device and technical information needed to run the service, such as network requests, IP address, app version, device type, and error logs created while operating the service.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage accounts and guest sessions.
  • Run multiplayer rooms, matchmaking, gameplay, and leaderboards.
  • Save progress, stats, friends, preferences, and rewards.
  • Send service messages and password reset messages.
  • Protect the game from fraud, abuse, cheating, and security threats.
  • Debug issues, improve reliability, and maintain the service.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate the game, including hosting, database, authentication, email delivery, app store, and security providers. These providers are allowed to use information only to provide services for Quiz Royale Showdown or as required by law.

Public Gameplay Information

Some gameplay information may be visible to other players, including display name, avatar or profile choices, presence, match status, scores, rankings, and leaderboard placement.

Children

Quiz Royale Showdown is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

Data Retention

We keep account, gameplay, and technical information for as long as needed to provide the game, maintain records, protect the service, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Guest session data may be deleted or expire sooner than registered account data.

Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect user information, including encrypted transport, hashed passwords, access controls, and monitoring. No online service can guarantee complete security.

Your Choices

You may choose not to create an account and may use guest features where available. You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting us.

Contact

For privacy questions or account deletion requests, contact: support@quizroyale.gg.
