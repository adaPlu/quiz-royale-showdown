Quiz Royale Showdown Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quiz Royale Showdown collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the mobile game, website, and related online services.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the game, we may collect:

  • Account information: username, email address, hashed password, account identifiers, authentication sessions, profile settings, and account entitlements.
  • Guest information: temporary guest identifier, display name, session credentials, expiration status, and guest gameplay progress.
  • Gameplay and social information: match participation, answers, scores, wins and losses, rankings, badges and progression counters, power-up use, friends, presence, seasonal progress, virtual-currency balances, cosmetics, and store inventory.
  • Purchase information: when you make an optional Google Play in-app purchase, the app sends the Google Play product identifier and purchase token to our server for verification. Google may also return transaction/order identifiers, quantity, and purchase time. We use a non-personal hashed account binding to associate the Play purchase with the correct Quiz Royale account.
  • Technical information: network requests, IP address and service logs needed for security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and reliable operation.

Payments

Optional purchases of coin or gem packs are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number, bank-account number, or other raw payment-method credentials. Our server verifies purchase status with Google Play before granting virtual currency.

For fraud prevention and duplicate-credit protection, we retain a cryptographic digest of the Google Play purchase token together with the associated account, product, quantity, granted virtual currency, transaction/order identifier when supplied by Google, and verification time. The raw purchase token is transmitted to Google for verification and finalization but is not stored in our purchase-receipt table.

How We Use Information

  • Create and manage accounts and guest sessions.
  • Run multiplayer rooms, matchmaking, gameplay, leaderboards, badges, seasons, and rewards.
  • Save progress, stats, friends, cosmetics, virtual currency, power-ups, and preferences.
  • Verify Google Play purchases, grant purchased virtual currency exactly once, and reconcile incomplete purchase finalization.
  • Send essential account messages such as password-reset messages.
  • Protect the game from fraud, abuse, cheating, and security threats.
  • Debug issues and maintain service reliability.

Service Providers and Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We use service providers to operate the game, including Cloudflare for web and real-time infrastructure, Railway and database/cache infrastructure for persistent services, and Google Play for Android distribution and in-app billing. These providers process information according to their own applicable terms and privacy commitments and as needed to provide their services or comply with law.

Public Gameplay Information

Some gameplay information may be visible to other players, including display name, profile or cosmetic choices, match state, scores, rankings, badges, and leaderboard placement. Presence information is used for social/game features.

Children

Quiz Royale Showdown is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate consent, contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

Data Retention

Registered account and gameplay data is retained while needed to provide the account and service, meet legal obligations, prevent fraud, and resolve disputes. Guest data may expire substantially sooner. Purchase-receipt and currency-ledger records may be retained as needed to prevent duplicate grants, support transactions, and meet accounting, fraud-prevention, or legal requirements.

Security

We use safeguards including encrypted network transport, password hashing, access controls, server-authoritative game outcomes, non-plaintext session-token storage, and idempotent purchase records. No online service can guarantee complete security.

Your Choices

You may use guest features without registering where available. You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting us. Some transaction records may need to be retained when required for fraud prevention, legal compliance, or transaction reconciliation.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game changes. The date at the top identifies the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, contact quizroyaleshowdown@gmail.com.
